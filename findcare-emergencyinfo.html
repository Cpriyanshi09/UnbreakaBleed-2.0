Emergency Information

Important emergency contacts and information for urgent medical situations.

Medical Emergency: If your experiencing a life threatening emergency, call 911 immediately. For urgent but not life-threatening situations, contact your healthcare provider or visit the nearest emergency room.

    When to seek Emergency Care

  • Severe chest pain or difficulty breathing

  • Signs of stroke (suddend weakness, confusion, speech problems)

  • Severe allergic reactions

  • Uncontrolled bleeding

  • Severe abdominal pain

  • High fever with severe symptoms

  • Loss of consciousness

  • Severe injury or trauma
