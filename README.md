# UnbreakaBleed-2.0

Your Companion in the Fight Against Breast Cancer

UnBreakaBleed is a digital healthcare platform designed to support women through every stage of the breast cancer journey -from awareness and early detection to emotional support and recovery. The platform combines AI-powered tools, verified healthcare resources, survivor communities, and personalized care assistance to make breast cancer support more accessible, informative, and compassionate.

By addressing challenges like lack of awareness, delayed diagnosis, financial barriers, and limited healthcare accessibility, UnBreakaBleed aims to create a safe and supportive ecosystem where no woman has to face breast cancer alone.

Key Features :

Symptom Checker
<br>
Guided symptom-based assessment system
Provides risk-based suggestions and next steps
Encourages early consultation and regular screening

AI Care Bot
<br>
24/7 AI-powered healthcare companion
Simplifies complex medical information
Offers emotional support and personalized guidance

Find Care
<br>
Helps users locate nearby verified hospitals, labs, and specialists
Includes accessibility support for rural and underserved areas
Enables faster connection to trusted healthcare services

Help Hub
<br>
Provides access to financial aid, NGOs, grants, and support programs
Connects patients with survivor communities and resources
Reduces confusion during treatment and recovery

Care Calendar
<br>
Personalized reminders for self-checks, screenings, medications, and appointments
Helps users maintain consistent healthcare routines
Reduces missed checkups and delayed care

Survivor Stories
<br>
Real stories from breast cancer survivors
Inspires hope, resilience, and emotional strength
Encourages awareness and breaks social stigma

Multi-language Accessibility
<br>
Supports users from diverse linguistic backgrounds
Makes healthcare information easier to understand
Promotes inclusivity and wider awareness reach

Tech Stack:

Frontend: React.js, Tailwind CSS
Backend: Node.js, Express.js
Database: MongoDB / PostgreSQL / Firebase
APIs & Services: OpenAI API, Google Maps API, GeoLocation API, Twilio, Translation APIs
Authentication & Security: Firebase Auth, JWT, AES Encryption
