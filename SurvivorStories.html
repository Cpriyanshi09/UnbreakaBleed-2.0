Lump to Breast Cancer | Triple Positive Breast Cancer | The Patient Story

By Alison
Alison discovered a lump during a postpartum checkup just 10 days after breastfeeding ended. Within 48 hours, she was diagnosed with stage 1A triple positive breast cancer. In this cancer vlog, she candidly describes the overwhelming emotions of facing a cancer diagnosis with two young kids, the tough decisions like undergoing a double mastectomy, and the grueling treatments including chemotherapy and immunotherapy.
